Gentle stretches that ease the pain 🧘

Simple poses you can do right in bed — tap a card to learn more

🧒

Child's Pose

Hold for 1-3 minutes

tap to expand ↓

1 Kneel on your bed or a soft surface with your knees slightly apart
2 Sit back on your heels and fold forward, resting your forehead down
3 Extend your arms forward or rest them alongside your body
4 Breathe deeply and let your lower back and tummy relax 💜
🐱

Cat-Cow Stretch

5-10 slow rounds

tap to expand ↓

1 Get on all fours with wrists under shoulders and knees under hips
2 Inhale: arch your back, lift your head (Cow) 🐮
3 Exhale: round your spine, tuck your chin (Cat) 🐱
4 Move slowly with your breath — this improves pelvic circulation ✨
🦵

Legs Up the Wall

Hold for 5-10 minutes

tap to expand ↓

1 Lie on your back next to a wall (or your headboard!)
2 Swing your legs up and rest them vertically against the wall
3 Let your arms rest by your sides, palms facing up
4 Close your eyes and breathe. This is deeply restorative 🌙
🦋

Reclining Butterfly

Hold for 3-5 minutes

tap to expand ↓

1 Lie on your back with a pillow under your head
2 Bring the soles of your feet together, let your knees fall open
3 Place pillows under your knees for support if needed
4 Place a warm compress on your tummy and just breathe 🦋
